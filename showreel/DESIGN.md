## Style Prompt
High-energy motion designer showreel built like a spatial broadcast package: deep black stage, impossible perspective, oversized kinetic typography, floating glass panels, chrome wireframes, sharp editorial framing, and punchy lens-like transitions. The work should feel authored, fast, and premium rather than template-driven.

## Colors
- #07070A — primary void
- #F4F0E8 — warm white typography
- #FF5C35 — kinetic orange accent
- #63E6FF — electric cyan depth cue
- #B8FF5A — signal green micro-accent

## Typography
- Arial Black / Impact for monumental display statements
- Courier New for technical labels and timing metadata

## Motion
- Fast confident entrances, 0.18–0.55 seconds
- Perspective pushes and lateral parallax for spatial depth
- Zoom-through and overexposure transitions as the primary language
- One calmer breath before the final identity lockup

## What NOT to Do
- No centered presentation-card layouts
- No generic rainbow gradients
- No tiny web typography
- No soft corporate easing throughout
- No stock footage or external images
