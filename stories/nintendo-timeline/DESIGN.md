## Style Prompt
A continuous historical visualization on deep brown handmade paper. One warm gold river of time connects Kyoto, the Eiffel Tower, handmade hanafuda and Nintendo hardware. Flowing points trace the same changing outlines: floral card → steel lattice → toy mechanism → consoles → floral card. Watercolor vignettes and hand-drawn mechanical objects, never a sequence of presentation cards. Restrained, warm, curious; no narration and no sentence-length captions.

- #8e9a78 — sage and screen phosphor

## Typography
Noto Serif CJK SC for Chinese locations and key phrases; Georgia for large years and Nintendo. All important type at least 30px at the 1920×1080 design scale. Large dates and titles remain readable over quiet areas of the paper.

## Motion
120 seconds, 16:9, 3840×2160, 24fps. Follow the document's detailed millisecond event table. The gold timeline stays present from 2s to the closing fade. A shared deterministic particle field changes shape continuously; objects never jump-cut. Use slow camera drift, organic ink reveals, smooth outline morphs and gentle dissolves. Historical scenes contain no modern devices. Eiffel Tower shown in daylight with no modern night lighting. Hanafuda floral patterns, never playing-card suits. Omit the disputed hotel story; Ultra Hand is a mechanical toy.

## Sound
Two versions with identical pictures and no narration. Music version: original gentle ambient piano, warm pads and restrained electronic pulses, plus sparse effects. No-music version: only the same sparse paper, brush, metallic wind and original synthesized startup effects. No sustained music or beat in the no-music version.

## What NOT to Do
- No long voiceover subtitles, chapter cards, neon effects, flashing or quick cuts.
- Do not replace morphs with a slideshow of static photographs.
- No modern hardware before its historical section.
- Do not present generated illustration as archival photography.
