## Style Prompt
A quiet historical watercolor picture book. Kyoto craft and Paris engineering share one year: 1889. Warm paper, faded pigments, museum captions, soft daylight, restrained documentary typography. Artwork carries each scene; dates are gentle editorial anchors. Only slow pushes, horizontal drift, fades and dissolves. Never substitute rapid kinetic graphics for the script's reflective pace.

## Colors
- #eee4d1 — aged ivory paper
- #292a27 — warm charcoal type and opening darkness
- #8d9b86 — dusty sage
- #a35e50 — faded brick date accents
- #596b78 — muted slate secondary information

## Typography
Noto Serif CJK SC (bookish Chinese narration and chapter titles), Georgia (large historical dates and Latin labels). Chinese captions use the same serif at a lighter weight, on a quiet ivory backing.

## Motion
120 seconds, 3840×2160, 24fps. 1.5–2 second dissolves, 3–5 percent slow picture pushes, restrained sideward drift. No cut, wipe, strobe or fast zoom. Intro and outro fade through charcoal. Each scene enters softly; the transition carries the preceding scene away.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon, oversaturated game UI, fast cuts, or flashing transitions.
- No modern game devices or Mario in the 1889 scenes.
- No illuminated modern Eiffel Tower; show original daytime reddish-brown iron structure.
- No playing-card suits or tarot cards in place of hanafuda.
- Do not present the disputed love-hotel story as established fact; omit it.
- Describe Ultra Hand as a mechanical toy, not an electronic toy.
